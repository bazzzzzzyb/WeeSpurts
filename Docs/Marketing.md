# Marketing & Launch

How friendslop games actually sell, condensed into a playbook. Marketing is not a Phase-7 activity — it's a *design constraint* from day one: **build moments worth clipping.**

## The one rule

~90% of new wishlists come from online festivals and streamer/creator coverage (Chris Zukowski's data), and this genre is consumed as TikTok clips and Twitch highlights first. So the marketing plan is: make the game produce 15–30 second clips, then put those clips where creators and the Steam algorithm can amplify them. Every feature gets the **clip test**: would you post 15 seconds of it?

## Timeline (relative to launch)

1. **When "first funny game" gate passes** — pay $100 Steam Direct, get the real App ID, put up the store page. Wishlists need 6–12 months of runway before launch; the page goes up early, not when the game is done.
2. **Page live →** post clips consistently (TikTok/Shorts/Reels + X). Chaotic physics fails outperform polished trailers in this genre. Tag #indiegame etc.; expect "showcase" creators to make content from your clips.
3. **Demo ready →** enter **Steam Next Fest** (runs Feb / Jun / Oct — pick the one you can hit with a *stable* 4-player demo). During the fest: daily clips, live streams on your own Steam page (live streams get 2–4× more page visits), and email outreach to creators in the 5k–250k follower range — they actively hunt Next Fest demos.
4. **Launch** — impulse pricing. Comps: PEAK $7.99, Lethal Company ~$9.99, R.E.P.O. ~$9.99, Golf With Your Friends $14.99 (2020). Sensible corridor: **$5.99–$9.99**, possibly with a launch discount and a 2-pack/4-pack bundle (friend games sell in packs — buying for your group is the point).
5. **Post-launch** — updates timed to Steam sales; a second minigame is a marketing beat, not just content.

## Steam content survey (gambling & drinking)

Valve requires honest disclosure of simulated gambling and alcohol/drug references; the rating impact is larger when the content is *central* to gameplay. Ours is: fake coins, no real money, satirical drink meter. Plan:

- Disclose everything honestly in the survey — undisclosed content is how pages get pulled; the disclosure itself is survivable.
- **Never** any path from real money to coins, and no cash-out. That's the line between "comedy gambling" and regulated gambling.
- Keep drinking cartoonish (the "drink" can even be ambiguous soda-chugging — decide at Phase 5; being one toggle away from a cleaner rating is cheap insurance).
- Before the store page: sanity-check PEGI/ESRB descriptors so a rating surprise doesn't hit at launch.

## Store page basics

Capsule art is the single highest-leverage image — commission it, don't AI-slop it (this genre's *games* can look cheap; its *capsules* don't). Short trailer (<60s) cut like the clips. Tags: Party Game, Sports, Physics, Online Co-Op, Funny, Multiplayer. Screenshots of chaos mid-moment, not empty lanes.
