# Vision

**Wee Spurts** = Wii Sports' readable sports + a "friendslop" chaos layer: exaggerated physics, heckling, fake side-bets, and a satirical drinking gimmick. Online, Steam-only, played in a lobby of friends.

Pitch: *Wii Sports if it egged you on to gamble on your friends and heckle their form.*

The emotional target is a specific memory: four friends on a call, one person lining up a bowling throw while the other three bet fake coins against them and trash-talk, then the physics do something stupid and everyone loses it. If a feature doesn't feed that moment, it probably doesn't belong.

First minigame: **Bowling** — chosen because it's near-turn-based, which makes online dramatically easier for a first netcode build, and its physics are simple enough to get "feel" right fast.

See `GameBible.md` for pillars and the full stack; `OpenQuestions.md` for what we're deliberately leaving undecided.
